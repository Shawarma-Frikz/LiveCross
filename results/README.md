# Results and Reproducibility

Store reproducible audit reports, experiment tables, plots, and failure-case summaries here. The tracked `audits/dot-sample-audit.json` is generated by `python scripts/audit_sample_data.py`; `audits/supplemental-dataset-audit.json` by `python scripts/audit_supplemental_data.py`; `audits/compact-subset-audit.json` by `python scripts/build_compact_subset_manifest.py`; and `audits/sequence-split-audit.json` by `python scripts/create_sequence_split.py`. These scripts inspect local files in Git-ignored `data/raw/` and download nothing.

The event annotation inventory is summarized by `annotation-summary-v1.json`, generated by `python scripts/summarize_event_annotations.py`. It reports split/category counts and durations, sample-gate status, uncertainty/exclusion examples, and reviewer coverage. Assistant first-pass annotations are not final ground truth and must be adjudicated before training or primary metrics.

For every reported experiment result, include the configuration, data/run IDs, split, random seed, model version, dependencies, hardware, and metric denominators. Keep raw video out of this folder and do not report event-level scores against run-level labels. Generated reports must distinguish automated measurements from human interpretation.
